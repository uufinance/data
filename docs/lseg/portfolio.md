# Portfolio

LSEG is essentially combining its security‑level data (prices, fundamentals, ESG, ownership, risk) into position‑ and portfolio‑level analytics. Here is what sits behind each submenu item.
Portfolio section of LSEG provides the following data:

- **Monitor** — LSEG ingests your positions (holdings, weights, trades, benchmarks) and overlays them with live and end‑of‑day market data to produce real‑time P&L, exposures, and performance by security, asset class, sector, region, and currency, with drill‑downs to the instrument level.
- **Watchlist Pulse** — For user‑defined lists of instruments or portfolios, LSEG streams live prices, volumes, news, events, and alerts, flagging unusual moves, crossing of thresholds (price, spread, rating, ESG), and key corporate or macro events that affect the list constituents.
- **Portfolio Analytics** — Here LSEG applies quantitative models to portfolio holdings: historical and factor‑based risk (volatility, tracking error, betas, style tilts), contribution‑to‑risk, performance attribution versus benchmarks, scenario and stress testing, and ESG or climate‑risk scores aggregated to the portfolio level.
- **Portfolio Dashboard** — This brings together summary views of portfolio composition, performance, risk, and constraints, combining tables and charts: top holdings, concentration measures, geographic and sector breakdowns, risk hotspots, liquidity profiles, and compliance/ESG indicators, all refreshed using LSEG’s underlying market, fundamental, and ESG data.
